Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Agnosia Team ("we", "us", or "Agnosia Team") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be involved when you use the Game and how Agnosia Team handles information made available to us.

Information We Handle

Agnosia uses certain platform features and online services to provide the Game. Information made available through those features may be processed as necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and support the Game.

Agnosia Team does not intentionally collect personal information that is not needed for the operation, support, security, or functionality of the Game.

Meta Platform Features

The following Meta platform features are currently active and are used by Agnosia:

  • User ID: Used to identify your Meta account within the Game and support account-related functionality.
  • User Profile: Used to access your Horizon username and profile photo for use in the Game.
  • User Age Group: Used to receive your age group information from Meta for age-related platform and Game functionality.
  • Friends: Used to access your Meta friends information where required for supported social and multiplayer features.
  • Blocked Users: Used to access your Meta block list so supported blocking and player-safety features can function.

The features listed above are currently active in the Game. Features that are not listed as active are not represented by this policy as currently being used by Agnosia.

Information provided through Meta platform features is subject to Meta's applicable privacy policies and platform terms.

Voice Communication

The Game may provide optional voice communication using your device's microphone. When you use voice communication, your audio is transmitted live so that other players can hear you.

Agnosia Team does not record, collect, store, or inspect your voice communications. Agnosia Team does not maintain voice recordings or voice-chat recordings on its own servers.

Voice communication is optional. You can control microphone access through your device or platform settings.

Third-Party Online Services

The Game uses third-party online services to provide multiplayer, networking, authentication, voice communication, platform features, purchases, and other online functionality.

Third-party providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Agnosia Team does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

Information You Provide to Us

If you contact Agnosia Team for support, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or other information included in your request.

We use information provided through support communications as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide assistance, maintain the Game, address technical or safety issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Community Communications

Communications between players may be visible or audible to other players. Do not share personal information that you do not want other players to receive.

Information you voluntarily post in public community spaces, including Discord or other third-party platforms, may be visible to other users and is subject to those platforms' own policies and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided directly to Agnosia Team. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Agnosia Team does not retain voice recordings or voice-chat recordings. Information provided directly to us through support communications may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including support, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

Children's Privacy

The Game may use Meta's age-group information where the applicable platform feature is enabled. We do not intentionally request or collect personal information from children beyond information made available through supported platform functionality or voluntarily provided to us.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Agnosia Team that should not have been provided, contact us so we can review the situation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
