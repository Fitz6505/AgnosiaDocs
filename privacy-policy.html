Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11/8/2025

Your Privacy Matters

At Agnosia Team, we take privacy seriously. Agnosia Team does not collect, retain, or distribute personal information about players beyond what is necessary to operate the Game on Meta platforms. When playing on Meta platforms, your data access is governed by Meta's privacy policies and the permissions you grant.

Microphone Access

The Game may ask permission to use your microphone only to enable live voice chat with other players.

Audio streams are routed directly between players; Agnosia Team does not persist or process voice recordings on our servers.

If you prefer, you can turn off microphone access through your device or platform privacy settings.

Third-Party Services

The Game relies on third-party online services to enable multiplayer and voice features. We use Photon for multiplayer connectivity and voice transport. Other platform services or providers may also be used for voice or networking. Please review the privacy policies and documentation of those third-party providers for details about how they handle data.

Agnosia Team does not retain or store voice streams that are transmitted between players through these third-party services.

Meta Platform Permissions

When playing this Game on Meta platforms, the following permissions and data access may be requested to enable core features and functionality:

  • User ID: Enables app access to a unique user identifier for various features.
  • User Profile: Provides access to your Horizon username and profile photo.
  • User Age Group: Allows the app to identify age group data to ensure age-appropriate content and features.
  • In-app Purchases and Downloadable Content: Enables purchase and content download functionality within the Game.
  • Friends List: Grants access to your friends list to enable certain platform features and social functionality.
  • Blocked Users: Provides access to your block list for moderation and user safety purposes.
  • Device Ban: Permits the app to enforce device bans for violations of these policies.

You can manage these permissions through your Meta device or platform privacy settings at any time. Agnosia Team will not use these permissions for any purpose other than providing the core functionality and features of the Game.
