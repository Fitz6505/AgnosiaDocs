Agnosia Team ("we", "us", or "Agnosia Team") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be involved when you use our game and how we handle that information.

Information We Collect

Agnosia is designed to minimize the personal information handled by Agnosia Team. We do not intentionally collect personal information that is not necessary to operate, maintain, support, or secure the game.

Information may be processed by the platforms and third-party online services required to provide features of the game. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Voice Communication

The game may provide optional voice communication using your device's microphone. When you use voice communication, your audio is transmitted in real time so that other players can hear you.

Agnosia Team does not record, collect, store, or inspect your voice communications. We do not maintain recordings or voice-chat logs on our servers.

Voice communication is optional. You can choose whether to use the microphone and whether to participate in voice communication.

The game may use third-party online services to provide multiplayer, authentication, networking, platform functionality, and other online features.

These third-party services may process technical information or other information necessary to provide their services. Communications made between players, including voice or other communications, may be heard or received by other players. You should avoid sharing personal information that you do not want other players to receive.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information that is provided directly to Agnosia Team. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Agnosia Team does not retain voice recordings or voice-chat recordings. Information provided directly to us through support or other communications may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, operate the game, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Children's Privacy
